Must-Visit Destinations

Highlights of Gianyar

Immerse yourself in serene natural views, rich artistic history, and timeless Balinese architecture.

01 / NATURE & WILDLIFE

Sacred Monkey Forest

Located in Ubud, this natural sanctuary shelters hundreds of long-tailed macaques alongside moss-covered ancient temples and giant banyan trees.

02 / SCENIC LANDSCAPE

Tegallalang Terraces

Famous for its dramatic step-like green rice fields, Tegallalang offers iconic panoramic views powered by Bali's traditional subak irrigation network.

03 / SACRED HERITAGE

Tirta Empul Temple

A holy spring water temple in Manukaya village where worshippers perform Melukat, a traditional Balinese ritual cleansing under freshwater spouts.

Visitor Etiquette

Tips for a Great Visit

  • ✓
    Dress Respectfully Wear a sarong and sash when entering holy temple compounds out of respect for local customs.
  • ✓
    Start Early Morning Visit popular spots like rice terraces early in the day to catch morning light and avoid afternoon heat.
  • ✓
    Mind the Wildlife Secure loose personal belongings (sunglasses, hats) when exploring monkey forest sanctuaries.

Exploring Gianyar Regency

Gianyar is celebrated worldwide as Bali's artistic hub, encompassing Ubud, Sukawati, Tegallalang, and Tampaksiring. Beyond famous landmarks, take time to visit local art markets, woodcarving villages, and traditional dance performances.
